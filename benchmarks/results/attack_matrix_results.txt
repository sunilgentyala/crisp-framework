Script      : benchmarks/sa_set_attack_matrix.py (n=100 trials per condition)

Scope and limitations:
  Protocol-level behavior of src/sa and src/set against genuine and
  adversarial inputs, with a fresh nonce and frame per trial. This is not a
  biometric accuracy measurement, not a liveness test, and not an evaluation
  against an adaptive adversary with access to the sensor or the TPM.
  A condition counts as "rejected" only when the verifier returned False or
  raised its documented rejection error; any other exception is reported
  separately as "error" (none occurred). AIK certificates in this run are self-signed
  demo certificates; the verifier trusts the pinned sensor public key. The hash+nonce and none columns are
  ablations showing what each check contributes. Intervals are exact
  Clopper-Pearson 95% bounds.
